When an Issue Is Found

Monitoring is about awareness. If an issue is identified, the next step is to determine what needs to be corrected and how that work should be handled.

  • Identify the affected website area
  • Review what appears to be happening
  • Communicate the issue clearly
  • Coordinate the appropriate follow up

Monitoring + Management

Monitoring and management work together. Monitoring helps identify what needs attention, while website management handles requested content and website changes.

  • Monitoring keeps you aware
  • Management handles updates
  • Issues can be addressed with a clear process
  • Standard management updates are completed within 24 hours after required information is received

Need an Update After a Monitoring Check?

If the website needs a content or page update, the Website Management service provides ongoing support. Standard update requests are completed within 24 hours after the required information is received.
