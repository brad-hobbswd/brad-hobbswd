Technology for Life

Technology should be useful, understandable, and accessible to the people who use it. Technology for Life is a separate educational resource I created to provide practical technology teaching materials for older adults and other everyday learners.

Learning for Older Adults

The resources are designed to make common technology tasks easier to understand, with clear information that can be approached one step at a time.

Practical Technology Skills

Explore educational material covering everyday digital tools, devices, online activities, and technology skills that can support greater confidence and independence.

Learning at Your Own Pace

Technology for Life is built as a resource you can return to as you learn, practice, and become more comfortable with technology.
