Technology Education

Learning Technology Should Not Feel Like a Race.

My degree in Computer Information Technology allows me to offer technology classes for older adults who may need additional time, repetition, and hands on guidance. Our world and society have changed rapidly, and smartphones, computers, online services, digital communication, and other technology are now part of everyday life. My approach is patient, practical, and focused on helping each learner understand technology at a pace that works for them.

Learn at Your Own PaceExtra time and repetition can be part of the learning process. The goal is understanding, not rushing through a lesson.
Practical TechnologyClasses can focus on devices, email, video calls, online services, passwords, privacy, and everyday digital tasks.
Build ConfidenceClear explanations and hands on practice can help learners become more comfortable using the technology around them.

Technology should be approachable at every age. I focus on clear explanations, patient instruction, practical examples, and opportunities to practice without feeling embarrassed for needing extra time.
