Why SEO Matters

Your Website Should Be Findable.

Search engines need to discover, crawl, understand, and index your pages before those pages can appear in search results. SEO support helps build the technical and content foundation that makes that process easier.

The Search Connection

Build the Foundation Before You Expect the Traffic.

Search visibility is not simply about adding keywords. It involves page structure, useful content, crawlability, indexing, internal links, sitemaps, metadata, and the overall quality of the experience.

Be DiscoverableHelp search engines discover important pages through logical structure, links, and XML sitemaps.
Be UnderstandableUse meaningful titles, descriptions, headings, content, and page structure so the purpose of each page is clear.
Be AccessibleAddress technical barriers that can interfere with crawling, indexing, or the visitor experience.
